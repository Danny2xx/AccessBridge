# Two-minute walkthrough

A script for the two-minute pitch video the UKREiiF brief asks for, and for live
demos. It follows The Story, then shows Explore and The Ask. The narration is
about 290 spoken words.

Figures are from the default plan on walking times routed along real streets.
If the data is rebuilt, take the new figures from the Evidence page.

## Before recording

- Start the backend and the site as described in the README, then open
  `http://127.0.0.1:5173/`.
- Use a browser window of about 1440 by 900 pixels.
- Move through the story with the right arrow key. Wait for the map to settle,
  about two seconds, before speaking over each step.
- Have `docs/screenshots/` open as a fallback if the live map is slow.

## Script

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | The Story, step 1: the Knowledge Quarter at street level | "Phase 1 of the Innovation Spine makes the walk from the city centre to the Birmingham Knowledge Quarter easy to see and follow. Phase 2 has ten million pounds to make it usable, including a loop of redesigned stops. AccessBridge asks one question: where should those stops go?" |
| 0:18 | Step 2: neighbourhoods coloured by deprivation | "About 350,000 people live around the Knowledge Quarter, in Nechells, Aston, Saltley and Small Heath. Seven in ten live in neighbourhoods among the most deprived ten per cent in England." |
| 0:32 | Step 3: the gap map | "Only about 48,000 of them can walk to a rail or Metro stop in ten minutes. Around 202,000 cannot." |
| 0:44 | Step 4: seven numbered stops and the route | "We gave an optimiser 828 possible stop locations from the national stop register, a budget of 600,000 pounds in placeholder costs, and a limit of eight stops. It counts the most deprived residents ten times as much as the least deprived. It chose seven stops." |
| 1:04 | Step 5: the 3D gain map | "Together they put about 104,000 of the most deprived residents within a ten-minute walk of a stop. That is 55,000 more than rail and Metro reach today, at about 11 pounds each." |
| 1:20 | Step 6: Mansel Road up close | "Mansel Road in Bordesley Green does the most. It reaches about 21,000 people, and none of them can walk to rail or Metro today." |
| 1:33 | Explore: drag the budget slider, then pick 15 minutes | "It is all live. Change the budget or the walking time and the plan re-forms in a second. Every walking time follows real streets, and every figure says whether it is measured data, our modelling or a placeholder." |
| 1:48 | The Ask | "Our ask is simple. Use this to place Phase 2's stops, then add bus and Metro journeys, real costs and residents' views before anything is built." |

## If there is more time

- **The Evidence page** shows what more budget buys. Reach overtakes today's
  rail and Metro reach at 375,000 pounds and stops rising at the 8-stop
  limit, 675,000 pounds.
- **The trade-off step** is the honest one. The plan aims at the most deprived
  first, so it reaches fewer people in less deprived areas than rail and Metro
  do today. Today's stations stay, so nobody loses what they have.
- **How it works** covers the four-step method and what the model does not
  include yet.
